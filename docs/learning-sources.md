## How far each source was read

- The full CommonMark and GFM pages were fetched in parts through a tool that answers questions about the page. The topics in the table were checked. Other sections were not reviewed line by line.
- The other pages were read for the topics in the table only.
- OpenAI, Anthropic and Ollama setup details were not checked against their current documentation.
- No live AI, Copilot or MCP service was used for this review.
